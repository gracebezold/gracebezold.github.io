About

I build machine learning systems that quantify behavior.

I'm an applied ML research scientist in the neuroscience research group at JHU/APL, where I work on a variety of projects using Computer Vision to enhance medical care. Alongside that I'm finishing a part-time M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Johns Hopkins.

Before APL I studied computer science and philosophy at the University of Notre Dame and did my undergraduate research in the Computer Vision Research Lab, building benchmarks and controlled experiments that isolate where facial recognition models fail.
